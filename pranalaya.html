Current Digital Presence Audit

Before we build, we need to be honest about where things stand. Pranalaya's existing digital presence has real strengths — and real gaps that are actively costing leads.

Website · pranaalaya.in

Critical
No SSL security warning visible on mobile browsers — The site runs on a shared WordPress multisite (note the /sites/30/ path in image URLs). This configuration often causes SSL certificate conflicts on subpaths, triggering "Not Secure" warnings that kill conversions for premium customers.
Critical
Elementor page builder bloat — Elementor 3.35.7 + external CSS + Google Fonts load generates 6–9 render-blocking scripts. PageSpeed score likely below 45 on mobile. Slow-loading wellness sites lose 53% of mobile visitors before they see the first treatment name.
High
WordPress multisite architecture risk — Running on a shared multisite (/sites/30/) means updates to any other site on the server can break pranaalaya.in. Plugin conflicts and hosting-level changes are outside your control.
High
No booking funnel clarity — "Book Appointment" and "Book Now" link to /contact-us/ which is a form, not a calendar. High-intent visitors (especially IT professionals who are time-pressured) expect instant slot selection. Every extra click is a lost booking.
High
Alaya (heritage stay) has no dedicated landing page — The most premium and differentiated asset — the palace-wood heritage home with 2 retreat rooms — is completely invisible on the website. This is the biggest missed revenue opportunity.
Medium
SEO-zero blog section — Blogs are listed in the nav but have no content visible. Ayurveda is one of the highest organic search intent categories in Trivandrum. Every month without blog content is surrendered traffic.
Medium

Medium
Products section — no pricing, no cart — The products page exists but has no visible prices, add-to-cart, or purchase flow. A product section that doesn't allow purchasing is a liability, not an asset. Either build it properly or remove it temporarily.
Low
Instagram (@pran.aalaya) started but thin — Presence is established but content cadence and content quality need significant elevation before paid ad traffic is directed there. A weak Instagram destroys the trust that Alaya's visual story could build.

WordPress — Why It Will Become a Problem

The WordPress Limitation Case

Speed
WordPress + Elementor sites regularly score 30–55 on Google PageSpeed Mobile. Core Web Vitals failure directly reduces Google ranking — for a business depending on "ayurveda near me" searches, this is a structural SEO penalty.
Security
WordPress powers 43% of all websites and is the most targeted CMS for cyberattacks. Plugin vulnerabilities, outdated themes, and shared hosting create regular security risks. One breach = customer data exposure = trust destroyed.
Scale
When the product line expands (3–4 products now, more coming), WordPress WooCommerce becomes unwieldy and expensive. Plugin costs, annual licence fees, and developer dependency compound every year.
Cost
Annual WordPress costs add up: premium theme (₹7–12K/yr), Elementor Pro (₹8K/yr), WooCommerce extensions (₹5–15K/yr), security plugin (₹5K/yr), maintenance (₹15–25K/yr). Total: ₹40–65K/year before hosting, for a site you don't own.
Future
Booking system integration, Alaya room availability calendar, product subscription, multilingual (for global wellness tourists) — all of these require custom development on WordPress anyway, at higher cost than building right the first time.

What They Have Right

Supriya's founder story is genuinely powerful — a personal healing journey that led to building a wellness center. Dr. Subha is a 4th-generation Ayurveda doctor with 20 years of experience. The Alaya heritage asset (palace-reclaimed wood, traditional architecture, 2 retreat rooms) is completely unique in Trivandrum. These are rare differentiators that most marketing has not yet touched. That's our starting point.

Content Strategy

Content is not posts. Content is the system by which Pranalaya becomes the most trusted Ayurveda name in Trivandrum. Every piece of content must serve one of three jobs: attract, trust-build, or convert.

Alaya — Content Pillars

Pillar 1The Heritage Story — The palace-wood home, the antiques, the handcrafted spaces. Visual storytelling reels. No captions needed sometimes — just beauty.
Pillar 2Founder's Vision — Supriya's personal healing journey. "Why I built Alaya." Short documentary-style video. This is the highest-converting content type for premium wellness brands.
Pillar 3Guest Transformation Stories — Testimonials, not reviews. "What changed for me after 3 days at Alaya." Real words from real guests. Instagram Stories + highlight reel.
Pillar 4The Ritual — Behind-the-scenes of Ayurvedic preparation: oils being prepared, morning routines at Alaya, the healing process. Makes audiences feel they're already there.

Pranalaya Center — Content Pillars

Pillar 1Dr. Subha's Expertise — Short educational reels: "What Panchakarma actually does to your body," "The Ayurvedic remedy for screen eye strain." Positions the doctor, not the clinic.
Pillar 2IT Professional Wellness — "3 Ayurvedic remedies every Technopark employee needs." Hyper-specific content that makes a segment feel seen. High share rate.
Pillar 3Package Explainers — "What happens in the De-Stress Package from morning to evening." Removes uncertainty (the #1 barrier to first bookings) and shows value clearly.
Pillar 4Patient Testimonials — Real patients, real results. Before-and-after (conditions only, not images if sensitive). Video testimonials are 4x more converting than text reviews for medical wellness.

The Content Hierarchy — What Comes First

Month 1

Foundation content only. Professional photography shoot at Alaya (the home, Dr. Subha, Supriya). This single shoot creates 3 months of visual content. No ads until photography is done — bad visuals for a premium brand is worse than no ads.

Month 2

Instagram and Google presence activated. GMB fully optimised with photos, services, and first 10 posts. Organic content cadence established. First WhatsApp lead generation ads for Technopark targeting.

Month 3+

Video content begins. Founder story video, Dr. Subha reels, first Alaya transformation testimonial. Product content begins only after treatment services have consistent lead flow. SEO blog publishing begins.

Website Plan

The current WordPress/Elementor site works for now — but it has a ceiling. If Pranalaya is planning product sales, Alaya room bookings, and a multi-brand presence, the right decision is building properly now rather than migrating under pressure later.

Option A · React / Next.js Custom Website

Recommended · Under ₹28,000 (basic)

SpeedNext.js / React sites score 85–95 on Google PageSpeed Mobile. The SEO advantage alone is worth the investment.
ScalabilityAlaya booking calendar, Pranalaya appointment system, product e-commerce, WhatsApp integration — all built natively, not via plugins.
Cost Over TimeZero annual plugin costs, no theme renewals, no Elementor licence. Server: ₹500–1,500/month (VPS). Total 3-year cost is lower than maintaining WordPress.
ControlYou own the code. If you change the agency, the site still works. No platform lock-in.
Products ReadyWhen the 3–4 Ayurvedic products expand to a full range, e-commerce is added as a module without rebuilding the entire site.
Investment₹25,000–₹30,000 basic site (5 pages: Home, Alaya, Pranalaya Center, Treatments, Contact + Booking). Server hosting ₹800–1,200/month additional. Akhil (FlumenX dev) can build and maintain this.

Option B · Continue with WordPress

Acceptable Short-Term · Problematic Long-Term

Risk 1Multisite architecture (/sites/30/) means your site performance is tied to other sites on the same server. An unrelated site crashing can pull Pranalaya offline.
Risk 2When product e-commerce is added, WooCommerce + Elementor together create severe performance degradation. Mobile PageSpeed can drop to 25–35.
Risk 3Annual costs: Elementor Pro (₹8K) + security (₹5K) + theme (₹8K) + maintenance dev hours (₹15–25K) = ₹36–46K/year for an increasingly slow site.
Risk 4Alaya booking integration — WordPress needs a premium booking plugin (₹5–12K/yr) that may not match Alaya's heritage visual aesthetic without heavy custom CSS.

If the decision is to stay on WordPress for now: at minimum, migrate away from the shared multisite to a dedicated WordPress install on a managed host (Cloudflare + SiteGround or WP Engine) — and add Alaya as a dedicated landing page immediately.

Immediate Website Fixes — Regardless of Platform Decision

Fix 1Replace /contact-us/ booking with a real calendar booking system (Calendly or Simplybook.me — both free tiers available). Connects directly to WhatsApp confirmation.
Fix 2Create a dedicated Alaya landing page — even a single, beautifully designed page with the heritage story, room details, package pricing, and WhatsApp booking link. This alone can generate direct revenue.
Fix 3Add Google Analytics 4 + Meta Pixel immediately. Without tracking, ad spend is blind. Every rupee spent on ads after this needs conversion data to optimise.
Fix 4Complete the GMB listing: add photos (interior, Dr. Subha, treatment rooms), services with pricing ranges, correct operating hours, WhatsApp number as primary contact.
Fix 5Products section: either add prices and a WhatsApp "order now" button (instant), or hide the page until proper e-commerce is ready. A products page with no purchase path is a dead end.
Fix 6Add schema markup for LocalBusiness + MedicalBusiness + Ayurvedic Wellness Center. This is a 30-minute technical task that significantly improves Google rich result eligibility.
